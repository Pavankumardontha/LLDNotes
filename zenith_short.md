Zenith is a platform for ticketed live events. Organizers sign up, create an event (a concert, a talk), describe it, set up the venue's seating and the 
prices, and publish it when ready. Buyers browse published events, pick seats, hold them briefly while they decide, pay, and receive tickets. At the venue,
each ticket is scanned at the gate and lets exactly one person in. And around that happy story live all the unhappy ones: holds expire, payments fail, 
events get cancelled or rescheduled, buyers ask for refunds, organizers edit details after publishing.
